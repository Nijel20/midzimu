A note on privacy

Stories and recordings are saved in this browser's local storage on this device. Nothing is uploaded or synced. Clear browser data and you may lose your archive, so use Save a backup to keep a copy somewhere you trust.

The optional AI helper sends story text to the Ollama service at the address shown in the form. Use a local Ollama instance; this app does not connect to a hosted AI service.
